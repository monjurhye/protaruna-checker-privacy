protaruna-checker – Privacy Policy

Developer: Pickixo · Last updated: 7 November 2026

protaruna-checker helps people in Bangladesh find out whether an SMS, WhatsApp message or screenshot is a scam. This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

1. Messages you check

When you tap "AI রিপোর্ট দেখুন", the message text, the sender you typed, and any screenshot you chose are sent over an encrypted connection to our server and then to OpenAI to produce the report. We do not keep your messages or screenshots. To avoid paying twice for the same scam SMS, the report for an identical text (not screenshots) may be kept in a temporary cache for up to 7 days, identified only by a one-way hash of the text. OpenAI processes the content under its API terms and does not use it to train its models.

2. Automatic warnings (notification access)

If you turn on automatic warnings, the app reads the text of new SMS and messaging-app notifications on your phone to look for scam signs. This check runs entirely on your phone. Notification content is never sent to our server unless you tap the warning and choose to get an AI report.

3. Scam-link list

The app downloads a list of known phishing domains from our server. When the AI rates a message as a high-risk scam, the web domains in that message (only the domain, such as example.xyz) are recorded together with a one-way hash of your device identifier, so that a domain reported from three different phones can be added to the list.

4. Device identifier and tokens

To keep your token balance, the app uses the Android ID that Android provides to this app, and on other platforms a random ID created by the app. It is used only to store your balance, prevent abuse (such as daily limits), and restore purchases. It is not linked to your name, phone number or email.

5. Purchases

Purchases (token packs and "remove ads") are handled by Google Play. We receive the purchase token and product ID to verify the purchase with Google and add it to your balance. We never see your payment details.

6. Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and app usage to show and measure ads, as described in Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads . The app also reports the estimated earnings of each ad (no personal data) to our server to calculate costs and profit. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.

7. What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not read your contacts, call logs or SMS inbox. We do not ask for your PIN, OTP or passwords — and you should never give them to anyone.

8. Data retention and deletion

Token balances and purchase records are kept while the app is in use. Usage totals are kept as anonymous daily numbers. To delete your balance and records, email us from the app's store page with the request; you can also uninstall the app at any time.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
